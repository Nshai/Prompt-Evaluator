### How your money is looked after

The People's Pension is governed by an independent corporate trustee, The People's Pension Trustee Ltd, which has responsibility for running the scheme and for looking after all the pots of money held by the scheme. The scheme is set up under a trust structure - this is intended to keep the scheme's assets separate from your employer and so provides you with a level of security. The trustee ensures that The People's Pension is run in the best interests of its members and other beneficiaries, and in accordance with the Rules of the Scheme and the law. It'll also make sure that the investment options are appropriate, and the administration is first class.

### Charges on your pension savings

The People's Pension's charging structure, known as the annual management charge (AMC), is made up of 3 elements:

1. The annual charge If you’ve been with The People’s Pension long enough, we’ll deduct the annual charge from your pension pot during the scheme year.

2. The management charge You'll pay a 0.5% management charge each year. That's just 50 pence a year for every £100 you have in your pension pot. The management charge covers the cost of investing your pension savings and running The People's Pension.

3. What you get back as a rebate To help you save more, typically you'll get a rebate on your management charge.

This all happens automatically and you'll be able to see the rebate in your Online Account and annual statement. If you take your money out, future rebates will be worked out on the value of any remaining savings. To qualify for a rebate, your money must be invested by the first day of a rebate period. So, when you first join, you won't receive a rebate at the end of the first rebate period. Your rebate might look like the example below. For the part of your savings:

- up to £3,000 no rebate is given
- over £3,000 and up to £10,000 we give back 0.1%\*
- over £10,000 and up to £25,000 we give back 0.2%
- over £25,000 and up to £50,000 we give back 0.25%
- over £50,000 we give back 0.3%. For more information, visit www.thepeoplespension.co.uk/charge

To find out exactly how much you're charged, please log into your Online Account at www.thepeoplespension.co.uk/viewaccount and go to 'Manage my pension', followed by 'Charges'.

### As you approach retirement…

If you're invested in one of The People's Pension's investment profiles, The People's Pension gradually starts to switch the investment of your savings 15 years before your selected retirement age. This is to more secure/lower risk investments (known as a 'glidepath' - you can visit www.thepeoplespension.co.uk/your-retirement-options to read more about this). It aims to limit any falls in the value of your pension savings just before you retire, but could also result in lower growth. If you choose to self-select, this option doesn't include a glidepath. You'll be responsible for managing how much you invest in each of your selected funds. You may want to consider reviewing both your fund selection and your attitude to investment risk regularly.

2. The second option is through an 'uncrystallised funds pension lump sum' (UFPLS). This involves spreading your tax-free cash across all withdrawals. Each time you take money from your pension pot, 25% of it is tax free. The other 75% is added to your taxable income. Depending on your tax status, parts of it may be liable for higher rates of tax.

# Combine your pensions

There's no charge from The People's Pension for transfers in or out of your account.

You could combine most previous pensions into the one you have with The People's Pension. Do this quickly and easily, with no charge from The People's Pension by visiting your Online Account.

The rate of the rebate on your management charge rises as your savings grow. You can boost your savings by transferring in any other pension pots you have. When you transfer in from another scheme, you may not be eligible to receive a rebate on the money transferred in until the next rebate period.

The People's Pension is part of the Origo Transfers Service. If your other pension scheme is too, funds can be transferred to or from a pension provider more quickly using this electronic service.

You'll need:

the name of your previous pension provider

the policy number for the pension you want to transfer

the approximate value of your other pensions.

Knowing this up front will help speed things up.

# Transfer to another provider

The value of your pension savings on the date of transfer is also the transfer value.

You're only able to transfer your whole pot to another provider - partial transfers aren't allowed.

For transfers of pots over £10,500, you'll need to provide proof of identity and address to The People's Pension.

You'll need The People's Pension account number for the pension you want to transfer - you'll find this in your Online Account.

This needs to be started by the pension scheme you're transferring to, so you should speak to them first to get this started.

People's Partnership are not able to give you any financial advice. Any information we provide about our products and services is given to help you make your own decisions based on your circumstances.
