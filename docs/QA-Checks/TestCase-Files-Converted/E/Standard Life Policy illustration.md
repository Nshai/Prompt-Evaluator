@ Important information  
It's essential for you to note that this illustration is based on a number of assumptions so your benefits at retirement  
could be very different. Tax rates or inflation rates could change and charges may vary. The value of investments can  
go down as well as up and you could get back less than you paid in.  
Inflation will make prices increase. When you retire things will be more expensive than they are today and you need to  
consider this when planning for your retirement. This illustration takes account of inflation.  
You will be sent a statement each year so you can keep track of your benefits.  
This illustration should be read along with the Group Flexible Retirement Plan Key Features Document.

Pension pot at retirement assumptions  
We've based the assumed returns on the investments you have chosen, allowing for charges, and we've reduced  
them by 2.0% to allow for the effect of inflation. The illustrated values allow for the charges that apply.  
We've assumed no more payments will be made.  
Please read ‘Information about tax relief, limits and your pension’.You can find this at  
standardlife.co.uk/taxandpensions, or phone us for a paper copy.

Annual income for life (an annuity) assumptions  
¢ You start taking a monthly pension the day you reach the retirement date stated on page 1  
¢ If you die within 5 years of taking your taxable annual income it will continue to be paid until the end of that 5 year  
period  
e Your pension payments will not increase  
e Your pension will not be paid to a dependant after you die  
¢ You can normally take up to 25% of your plan value as a tax free lump sum. If you did this your annual income  
would be reduced by 25%. The illustrated values given in this quote assume you do not take a 25% tax free lump  
sum  
Your plan gives you options when you retire. We've illustrated what the benefits might be if you choose to buy a  
taxable annual income using your full fund value however, other options are available.
