Investment objective
The People's Pension Global Investments (up to 85% Shares) Fund aims to
achieve long-term capital growth by investing in a range of asset classes in
the UK and overseas. These can include, but are not limited to, equities,
government bonds, corporate bonds. The fund is medium / high risk and will
typically hold up to 85% in equities, with a mix of UK and overseas equities.
