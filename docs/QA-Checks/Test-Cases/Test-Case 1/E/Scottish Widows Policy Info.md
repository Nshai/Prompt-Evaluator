## Yearly Scottish Widows fund based charge

We take a fund based charge calculated on the value of each of your investments. The charge is calculated daily and taken each month by selling units or shares held in line with the disinvestment strategy outlined below.

## AMC - Annual management charge

This is a charge applied by fund managers to cover the cost of investment management and administration. The charge is deducted from the fund every day before unit prices are calculated.

## Fund expenses

These are deducted from the funds before the unit prices are calculated. They are additional expenses in the day-to-day management of the funds' activities and include expenses, taxes, duties and other charges incurred in the purchase, sale, valuation and maintenance of the investments as well as auditors' and custodians' fees.

Fund expenses are not fixed. The figures shown are estimates based on what the fund expense has been in the past. The actual expense could be more or less than this.

## Disinvestment strategy

Although charges are calculated based on the value of individual investments, the actual charge is taken from your investments in a specified order.
