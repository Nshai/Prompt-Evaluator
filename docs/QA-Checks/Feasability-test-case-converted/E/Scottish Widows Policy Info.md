Yearly Scottish Widows fund based charge

We take a fund based charge calculated on the value of each of your investments. The charge is calculated daily and taken each month by selling units or shares held in line
with the disinvestment strategy outlined below.

AMC - Annual management charge

This is a charge applied by fund managers to cover the cost of investment management and administration. The charge is deducted from the fund every day before unit prices
are calculated.

Fund expenses

These are deducted from the funds before the unit prices are calculated. They are additional expenses in the day-to-day management of the funds' activities and include
expenses, taxes, duties and other charges incurred in the purchase, sale, valuation and maintenance of the investments as well as auditors' and custodians' fees.

Fund expenses are not fixed. The figures shown are estimates based on what the fund expense has been in the past. The actual expense could be more or less than this.

Total expense ratio

This is the total of the fund expenses and the annual management charge less the annual management charge rebate.

Cost of investment

This is the total of the yearly Scottish Widows fund based charge, Intermediary charges and total expense ratio.

'Cost of investment' may also be referred to as 'Total Annual Fund Charge'.

Disinvestment strategy

Although charges are calculated based on the value of individual investments, the actual charge is taken from your investments in a specified order. Charges will be taken from
cash held in your account then, if there isn't enough cash to cover the charges, from the least volatile fund you are invested in.
