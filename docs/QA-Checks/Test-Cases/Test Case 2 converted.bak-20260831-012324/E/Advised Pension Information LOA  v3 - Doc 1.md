## Plan Details

- No annuity options
- Allocation rate is 100%.
- The Pension input period is Tax year to Tax year.
- No guaranteed annuity rates
- No guaranteed minimum pension
- 25% Tax free cash, 75% Taxable flexible drawdown available
- No Terminal bonuses
- We have over 3000 funds with more than 110 fund managers.
- We don't have a hard limit of how many funds the client can have but we advise a soft limit of 50.
- Transfers can be accepted from other schemes, providing they meet our criteria
- More than 25% tax-free cash can be provided where the client has existing entitlement, providing that legal requirements are met. [Any transfer with protected TFC must meet block transfer requirements, and all benefits must be crystallised in a single transaction.]
- We do offer full and partial encashment from age 55. We offer flexi-access drawdown.
- Bid/Offer Spread: This can be identified on the Aviva for Advisers fund centre by using the fields Buying Price and Selling Price.
- With Profits funds are not applicable to this plan.
