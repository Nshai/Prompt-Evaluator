- The growth rates are not guaranteed. They are not minimum or maximum amounts. What you get back will depend on what you make on your actual investments. The value of your pension fund can go up or down and may be worth less than has been paid in.
- While only a selection is shown the projections have been carried out for an income assumed payable throughout the term of the portfolio.
- Product and Adviser charges have been assumed to remain the same as they are today.
- However, charges may vary in the future.
- Price inflation is the general increase in the price of goods and services over time, it's important to note that price inflation reduces the worth of all savings and investments. These projections illustrate what you might get back in 'today's money' which means it takes the effect of price inflation at 2.00% into account.
