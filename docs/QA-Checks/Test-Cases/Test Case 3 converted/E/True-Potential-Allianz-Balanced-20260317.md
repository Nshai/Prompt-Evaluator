## February Update

Global equities delivered further gains in February as value style shares significantly outperformed their growth style peers. Emerging Market (EM) equities outpaced their developed market counterparts despite weakness in China. In developed markets, Japanese shares led the gains, while US equities proved a laggard. In fixed income, long-dated UK Gilts outperformed amid lower yields.

The True Potential Allianz Balanced Fund returned 2.80% (net of fees). The Fund's large allocation to equities proved advantageous, with exposures to Japanese and EM shares driving performance over the period. The allocation to UK equities via the iShares Core FTSE 100 ETF was another notable contributor. Conversely, the overweight to US equities against the strategic asset allocation (SAA) detracted, with the Invesco S&amp;P 500 Fund (hedged to sterling) and the Amundi S&amp;P 500 II ETF delivering negative returns. Elsewhere, a small allocation to Hong Kong-based technology shares was the biggest laggard over the period. The fixed income segment produced gains overall, supported by strong returns from UK Gilts and EM debt. A sizeable allocation to the Xtrackers Global Government Bond Fund (hedged to sterling) also performed well. The alternatives segment produced gains overall, driven by strong returns from gold and a modest exposure to the Allianz Multi Asset Long/Short Fund.
