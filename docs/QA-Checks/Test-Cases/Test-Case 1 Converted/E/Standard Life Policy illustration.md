## Annual income for life (an annuity) assumptions

- You start taking a monthly pension the day you reach the retirement date stated on page 1
- If you die within 5 years of taking your taxable annual income it will continue to be paid until the end of that 5 year period
- Your pension payments will not increase
- Your pension will not be paid to a dependant after you die
- You can normally take up to 25% of your plan value as a tax free lump sum. If you did this your annual income would be reduced by25%. The illustrated values given in this quote assume you do not take a 25% tax free lump sum

Your plan gives you options when you retire. We've illustrated what the benefits might be if you choose to buy a taxable annual income using your full fundvalue however, other options are available.
